# Corvallis School District 509j: CTE Alignment & Optimization Brief
**Crescent Valley High School (CVHS) & Corvallis High School (CHS)**

> **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front):** 
> To combat the dual challenges of population contraction and enrollment shifts, CSD 509j must optimize its Career & Technical Education (CTE) offerings across CVHS and CHS. This brief outlines a "Top 2% Consulting" methodology, leveraging a distributed AI Agent Swarm to rigorously parse course descriptions, lengths, teacher pathways, and state standards. By cross-examining this internal data against external labor market demands and post-secondary outcomes, we will identify alignment gaps, eliminate redundant overlaps, and deliver a precise, actionable restructuring roadmap ready for leadership review.

---

## 1. The "Top 2%" Consulting Methodology
Average consultants merely map existing courses to standard state pathways. The **top 2%** look at systemic efficiency, economic viability, and future-proofing. Here is what we do differently when hired for a district-wide CTE audit:

*   **Vetting for Real-World Friction Outcomes:** We evaluate not just what happens in the classroom, but whether the skills and industry certifications earned are actively sought by regional employers (e.g., Willamette Valley/Oregon tech and trades labor data).
*   **De-duplication & Shadow Curricula Analysis:** We don't just look at official course titles. We analyze the descriptions and syllabi to find "Shadow Overlap"—where different teachers at CVHS and CHS are teaching 60% of the same foundational skills under different pathway names, costing the district FTE efficiency.
*   **Forensic Gap Discovery ("What we don't know we don't know"):** We look at what adjacent, economically thriving districts are doing in CTE that Corvallis is entirely missing (e.g., advanced automation, modern health sciences, green tech).
*   **Ruthless Prioritization:** We deliver a divest/invest matrix to help the district cleanly decide which pathways to sunset and where to cleanly re-allocate funding for maximum enrollment impact.

## 2. Project Scope & Objectives

**Target Campuses:** Crescent Valley High School (CVHS) & Corvallis High School (CHS).
**Primary Objective:** Map, align, and optimize CTE pathways to maximize student outcomes while mitigating the frictional costs tied to population and enrollment contraction.

**Key Deliverables Generated by the Swarm:**
1.  **Course Typology & Alignment Map:** Absolute visual mapping of all CTE courses (length, description, teacher, pathway, prerequisites) at both CVHS and CHS.
2.  **Redundancy vs. Synergy Report:** Clear identification of where dual-campus offerings are necessary vs. where they cannibalize each other's enrollment.
3.  **Labor Market Viability Index:** Scoring every pathway against 5-10 year regional economic forecasts.
4.  **Executive Restructuring Playbook:** The definitive PDF roadmap for 509j leadership.

## 3. Agent Swarm Architecture (Distributed Analysis)

To efficiently process the unstructured, disparate data of two high schools, we will deploy specialized AI agents with narrow, deep mandates:

### ⚙️ Agent 1: Data Aggregation & Intake
*   **Focus:** Ingest all course catalogs, master schedules, course lengths (e.g., semester vs. year), and syllabus texts.
*   **Vetting Protocol:** Cross-reference published catalog descriptions against what is actually being taught across the separate pathways to flag outdated documentation.

### ⚙️ Agent 2: Standard & Pathway Alignment
*   **Focus:** Match CVHS and CHS courses to current Oregon Department of Education (ODE) CTE standards.
*   **Output:** Flag courses that are "Orphans" (lacking clear upward pathway progression) or "Bottlenecks" (where advanced courses lack sufficient intro-level funnels).

### ⚙️ Agent 3: Redundancy & Efficiency Analytics
*   **Focus:** Compare CVHS and CHS offerings side-by-side.
*   **Output:** Identify where consolidation of a pathway to a single campus (or a centralized hub model) saves resources without devastating enrollment.

### ⚙️ Agent 4: Industry & Market Relevance
*   **Focus:** Validate current pathways against Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) and Oregon Employment Department projections.
*   **Output:** Pass/Fail/Pivot recommendations for legacy programs.

## 5. Guardrails & Success Metrics

### Success Metrics
*   **Completeness:** 100% of current CTE courses mapped, categorized, and scored.
*   **Actionability:** Delivery of exactly 3 distinct scenarios for restructuring (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive).
*   **Clarity:** A polished, BLUF-driven PDF executive summary that requires zero translation for non-technical district leadership.

### Guardrails (What NOT to do)
*   **No Teacher Evaluation:** This is a strictly objective audit of *systems, alignment, and pathways*, not individual teacher performance.
*   **No Generic Solutions:** Recommendations must be constrained by the reality of 509j's physical footprint, local transportation logistics between CVHS/CHS, and state funding mechanics (e.g., Perkins V).
*   **Bias Mitigation:** Ensure CHS and CVHS are weighted equally in analysis; avoid prioritizing one campus's legacy programs blindly due to historical precedence.

---

## 6. Dealing with Ambiguity

When data is missing or contradictory (e.g., a teacher's localized course structure doesn't match the official school catalog):
1.  **Flag for Human Review:** The swarm will isolate the discrepancy in a cleanly formatted "Anomalies" table.
2.  **Assume the Catalog Baseline:** For structural modeling, the official catalog will be used as the source of truth for FTE and credit calculations, while noting the pedagogical variance.
3.  **The "Blank Slate" Protocol:** If a pathway's actual purpose or alignment is highly ambiguous, the swarm will analyze it as if it were a newly proposed course, evaluating its existence strictly on market need and cost to run relative to district contraction.

## 7. College Now (LBCC) Dual-Credit Integration Strategy

A critical layer of the CTE alignment is maximizing frictionless transitions to post-secondary education. The swarm will explicitly analyze the district's integration with **Linn-Benton Community College's (LBCC) "College Now"** program.

**Current Baseline Assessment (CHS vs. CVHS):**
Based on the current LBCC dual-credit data, both campuses heavily skew toward core academics (Math, Spanish, History, English). 
*   **CVHS Current State:** CVHS offerings are currently limited to core academics, Ceramics, Physics, and basic Health/PE (First Aid/CPR). 
*   **The Gap:** There is a glaring absence of articulated "Hard CTE" dual-credit pathways (e.g., Computer Science, Engineering/Manufacturing, advanced Health Sciences, or Business).

**The Swarm's "College Now" Mandate for CVHS:**
When assessing CVHS's distinct CTE identity, the Swarm will map out **Dual-Credit Expansion Vectors**. Instead of just looking at what high school credits are earned, the swarm will map which current or proposed CVHS CTE courses can be easily credentialed by LBCC. 

**Deliverables for this Sub-Section:**
1.  **Syllabus Crosswalk:** The swarm will automatically compare CVHS CTE syllabi with introductory LBCC trade/tech syllabi to find 80%+ matches where articulation agreements should be actively pursued.
2.  **Prioritized Dual-Credit Roadmap:** Identifying the top 3 CTE courses at CVHS that should be fast-tracked for "College Now" approval to create immediate value for students and drive enrollment.

# District & Administrative Executive Summary: CTE Optimization Phase I
**Corvallis School District 509j: Strategic CTE Alignment (CVHS & CHS)**

> **Objective:** To provide CSD 509j District Administrators and the School Board with a clear, data-driven rationale for the restructuring of CTE pathways. This document translates the findings of the Agent Swarm and Teacher Alignment Summits into actionable policy and funding decisions.

---

## 1. The Strategic Imperative: Why Now?
As our district faces population contraction and enrollment shifts, we cannot sustain parallel, redundant programs across Crescent Valley High School (CVHS) and Corvallis High School (CHS). 

*   **The Problem Address:** Duplication of foundational courses drains Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) resources, preventing the district from offering advanced, specialized capstone courses that actually lead to high-wage outcomes.
*   **The Opportunity:** By aligning our curriculum, standardizing course descriptions, and ruthlessly prioritizing pathways tied to regional economic demand (Willamette Valley Tech/Trades), we can maintain a robust CTE ecosystem with a leaner demographic footprint.

---

## 2. The Restructuring Framework: The "Hub and Spoke" Model
To guarantee equity of access while maximizing efficiency, CSD 509j will adopt a modified Hub and Spoke model for expensive, high-level CTE programs:

*   **The Introductory Phase (Level 1/2):** Both CVHS and CHS will maintain identical foundational courses (e.g., Intro to Computer Science, Intro to Manufacturing) to ensure broad access for underclassmen at their home campus.
*   **The Capstone Hubs (Level 3/4):** Advanced courses with high equipment/facility overhead will be centralized. For example, if CHS has superior culinary facilities, the advanced culinary capstone exists *only* at CHS. CVHS students on that pathway utilize district-provided transportation during a specialized block schedule to attend the capstone.

---

## 3. Trade-offs and Institutional Impact

### Trade-off 1: Master Schedule Complexity
*   **The Reality:** The "Hub and Spoke" approach requires the district to align the master bell schedules of CVHS and CHS perfectly for the advanced CTE blocks (e.g., periods 6/7 must align). 
*   **The Benefit:** We consolidate two half-full, expensive advanced courses into one robust, fully enrolled master class, saving an entire FTE section.

### Trade-off 2: Legacy Program Sunsetting
*   **The Reality:** We must sunset historically beloved but economically irrelevant pathways to fund modern programs (e.g., phasing out an outdated woods program to fund an advanced automation/robotics lab).
*   **The Benefit:** We align district outputs with modern Linn-Benton Community College (LBCC) and Oregon State University (OSU) articulations.

### Trade-off 3: Teacher Reassignment vs. Reduction
*   **The Reality:** Aligning courses means some teachers may lose their hyper-niche, localized class and be required to teach the standardized district curriculum.
*   **The Benefit:** We create unified Professional Learning Communities (PLCs) where a CVHS teacher and a CHS teacher are instructing the exact same material, allowing for shared grading rubrics and collaborative lesson planning.

---

## 4. Measurable Return on Investment (ROI) Metrics

The District Office will measure the success of the CTE alignment over a 36-month horizon using the following hardened metrics:

1.  **Efficiency Node (FTE Optimization):** A 25% reduction in CTE sections running with fewer than 15 students enrolled.
2.  **Articulation Node (Dual-Credit):** A 40% aggregate increase in LBCC "College Now" credits awarded to CSD 509j students in designated "Hard CTE" pathways. 
3.  **Equity Node (Cross-Campus Access):** Track the raw number of students crossing campuses for capstone courses. An initial baseline of 50 students per semester utilizing the Hub and Spoke model is the Year 1 target.
4.  **Economic Node (Perkins V Funding):** Alignment of 100% of remaining courses to recognized Programs of Study to ensure total optimization of state and federal CTE funding streams.
